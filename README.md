DermaLens AI 

AI-Based Skin Lesion Analysis


DermaLens AI is an educational AI prototype designed to analyze skin lesion images and explore AI-based classification. It aims to promote awareness through image analysis and explainable AI techniques such as Grad-CAM. This student project explores the application of artificial intelligence in healthcare.

Objectives

- Explore AI-based skin lesion image classification.
- Understand the role of AI in healthcare.
- Learn about explainable AI techniques.
- Promote awareness of responsible AI use in healthcare.

Technologies Used

- Python
- Google Colab
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Streamlit 
- Grad-CAM 


Disclaimer

DermaLens AI is intended for educational and research purposes only. It is not a medical diagnostic tool and must not be used to make medical decisions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.
